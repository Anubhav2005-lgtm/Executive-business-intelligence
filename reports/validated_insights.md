## Findings

### 1. Electronics and Mobile are the biggest revenue drivers, but have the weakest margins
Electronics generated **17.88M** in revenue (31.1% of valid revenue) at a **16.98%** margin. Mobile generated **16.16M** (28.1%) at only **13.97%** margin. Together, these categories represent **59.3%** of valid revenue.

**Management implication:** prioritize pricing, discount and supplier-cost reviews in Electronics and Mobile before simply pursuing additional volume.

### 2. Furniture combines meaningful scale with strong profitability
Furniture generated **13.07M** in revenue and a **25.01%** margin, materially above the overall company margin.

**Management implication:** investigate whether successful pricing, product mix or lower discounting in Furniture can be replicated in weaker-margin categories.

### 3. West is the largest region by revenue
West generated **14.22M** in revenue, the highest of the five regions. Regional margins are tightly clustered around the company average, so revenue scale—not a dramatic profitability gap—is the main differentiator.

**Management implication:** protect the West customer base while looking for operational improvements that can lift smaller regions without sacrificing margin.

### 4. Online is the dominant sales channel
Online generated **31.54M**, approximately **54.9%** of valid revenue.

**Management implication:** online conversion, retention and merchandising improvements have the largest potential company-wide impact.

### 5. Customer segments have similar revenue productivity
Consumer is the largest segment by total revenue, while revenue per customer is broadly similar across Consumer, Corporate and Small Business.

**Management implication:** avoid treating total segment revenue as customer quality by itself; use retention, order frequency and margin per customer in the next analytical iteration.

## Data Quality
- 120,000 transaction rows were analyzed.
- No duplicate Order IDs were detected.
- 960 Customer Satisfaction values are missing.
- 3,455 orders are cancelled and excluded from operating KPIs.
- No negative revenue values were detected.
- No out-of-range Profit Margin values were detected.

## Recommended Management Actions
1. Review discounting and procurement economics in Mobile and Electronics.
2. Study Furniture's higher margin structure for transferable practices.
3. Prioritize online-channel optimization because it carries the largest revenue base.
4. Add customer retention/frequency metrics before making major segment investment decisions.
5. Monitor cancellations and returns separately from operating sales KPIs.
